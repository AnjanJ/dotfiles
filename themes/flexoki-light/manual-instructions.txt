  Manual Theme Steps: Flexoki Light
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

macOS appearance was switched to Light (dotfiles toggle appearance off
to leave it alone). The following apps need manual theme selection.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SLACK
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Open Slack → Preferences → Themes
  2. Scroll to "Colors" section and click "Custom Theme"
  3. Paste this color string:
     #fffcf0,#f2f0e5,#205ea6,#fffcf0,#e6e4d9,#100f0f,#66800b,#af3029,#fffcf0,#100f0f

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OBSIDIAN / BROWSERS / RAYCAST
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Flexoki started as an Obsidian theme: Settings → Appearance → Themes → "Flexoki".
  Chrome and Firefox have no official Flexoki theme; the light system
  appearance is enough for both. Raycast: search "Flexoki" at
  https://ray.so/themes/ and click "Add to Raycast".
